# Dish photography — sources and licences

Every photograph here came from Wikimedia Commons and was **looked at** before being put on
the menu, not accepted on the strength of its filename. Commons was chosen over a stock site
because the licence and the author come back as structured data, so the attribution below is
recorded rather than guessed at.

Files are ~1400px thumbnails, not originals: the originals run to several megabytes and the
upload endpoint caps at 2 MB.

**Reusing these:** most are share-alike or attribution licences. If any of this is published
beyond a course project, the credit below has to travel with the image.
